🥗 Diet & Nutrition

Build balanced meals and support your fitness journey.

🌅

Breakfast

Oats with milk, eggs with whole-grain toast, or idli with sambar.

🍱

Lunch

Rice or roti, dal, vegetables, and a protein source such as eggs, beans, paneer or chicken.

🍎

Snack

Fruit, nuts, roasted chana, or yogurt if you enjoy and tolerate it.

🌙

Dinner

Choose a balanced meal with vegetables, protein, and grains according to your hunger.

Daily Nutrition Tips

  • Include protein in your meals.
  • Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables.
  • Drink water regularly throughout the day.
  • Adjust meal portions to your needs and activity.

These are general ideas, not a personalized medical diet. Your needs depend on age, activity, health and goals.
