🏋️ Exercise Library

Explore beginner-friendly movements. Start gently and use good form.

🦵

Squats

Targets legs and glutes. Keep feet stable and move within a comfortable range.

Starter idea: 2 sets of 8 reps.

💪

Wall Push-ups

Beginner-friendly upper-body exercise. Keep your body aligned as you bend your elbows.

Starter idea: 2 sets of 8 reps.

🚶

Walking

A simple way to move more. Choose a pace and duration that feel comfortable.

Starter idea: 10–20 minutes.

🧘

Stretching

Gentle stretches can help mobility. Avoid bouncing or forcing a stretch.

Starter idea: 5 minutes.

🪑

Chair Sit-to-Stand

Stand up from a sturdy chair and sit down slowly, using support if needed.

Starter idea: 2 sets of 8 reps.

🛌

Glute Bridges

Lie on your back with knees bent and lift your hips gently while keeping your feet planted.

Starter idea: 2 sets of 8 reps.

These are example starting points, not a personalized training prescription. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or unusual shortness of breath.
